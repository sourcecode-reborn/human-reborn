## V. Foundational Question Four: Why Do All "Ancient Scripts" Share the Same Underlying Logic?

**The textbooks tell us:**

**Stage One: Knot records / carved symbols.**
- China: Fuxi "governed with knotted cords" — archaeology has found Jiahu carved symbols (8,000 years ago).
- Sumer: Primitive counting "tokens" — the earliest written recording system.
- Inca: Quipu — knot records — used until the 16th century.

**Stage Two: Pictographic writing (images directly represent things).**
- China: Oracle bone script — sun, moon, mountain, water — literally pictures.
- Egypt: Hieroglyphs — also pictures.
- Sumer: Early cuneiform — still pictures.

**Stage Three: Ideographic (extended meaning) + phonetic (borrowed sound).**
- China: Phono-semantic compounds — 江 (river) = 水 (water radical) + 工 (sound).
- Egypt: Syllabic signs in hieroglyphs — representing syllables/consonants.
- Sumer: Syllabic signs in cuneiform — representing syllables.

**Stage Four: Alphabetization (pure phonetics — completely separated from images).**
- Proto-Sinaitic → Phoenician → Greek → Latin alphabet.
- This "alphabetization" process — is a **globally universal writing evolution path.**

**The question: How can "independently invented" systems possibly share the exact same "evolutionary stages"?**
